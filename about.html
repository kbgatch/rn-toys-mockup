The shop

A bench in Auckland, with a name you can put on a parcel.

RN Toys Collectibles NZ started the way a lot of good New Zealand shops do: one genuine sale at a time, in a Facebook group and on Trade Me. The cards were already real. The missing piece was a shelf that didn't disappear when a post scrolled away.

We're NZ-owned and we pack from Auckland. Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, the One Piece Card Game and Lorcana sit next to toys and the sleeves that keep a pull alive. If we wouldn't buy it ourselves, it doesn't get a listing.

The mark

The picture mark is a bear face on a red square, with a small gold chevron. It stays clear at the size of a favicon, a sleeve stamp and the corner of a mailer. RN Toys Collectibles NZ does the explaining: what the shelf holds, and which country it ships from.

What a price means here

Every figure is NZD and already includes GST. You shouldn't need a calculator to know what leaves your account. Afterpay belongs on the live checkout for the days a booster box is a big swing. Sample postage on this mockup is $7.50, and free once an order reaches $150.

Pre-orders and the next set

New sets sell out in the group before some people have seen the post. A pre-order listing, and a short alert when stock lands back in Auckland, is the calmer version of that. The signup on the home page shows the shape. It doesn't keep your email.

This page is a proposal

The words above are the about-page copy we'd put on the live Shopify theme. The catalogue behind them is sample data: fictional set names, placeholder art, and prices chosen to show the range, not today's market.
